It should be possible to capture almost everything except fraction denominators with linearized formulas, and this lets us use LP. We can design a series of formulas with multiples limits, and this results in diminishing returns.

Suppose we add a new layer of indirection: prosperity points aka fitness points aka pp.

## Goal: produce sellable goods

- Captures the first 100%: 1090 $/min -> 1000 pp (max 1 multiples)
- 1090 $/min -> 100 pp (max 0.2 multiples)
- 1090 $/min -> 50 pp (max 0.8 multiples)
- 1090 $/min -> 10 pp

## Goal: power our own factory

- Captures the first 100%: 7000 W -> 1000 pp (max 1 multiples)
- 7000 W -> 100 pp (max 0.05 multiples)
- 7000 W -> 10 pp

## Goal: produce excess goods for delivery jobs

- any material -> Generic Material
- Captures the first 100%: Generic Material 28000/day -> 1000 pp (max 1 multiples)
- Generic Material 28000/day -> 100 pp (max 0.5 multiples)
- Generic Material 28000/day -> 10 pp

## Goal: produce crafting ingredients

- Hetonite Part 0.1/min -> 500 pp (max 1 multiples)
- Hetonite Part 1/min -> 100 pp (max 1 multiples)
- any Component -> Generic Component
- Generic Component 0.1/min -> 500 pp (max 1 multiples)
- Generic Component 1/min -> 100 pp (max 1 multiples)
- Generic Component 1/min -> 10 pp
- Xiranite Component 0.1/min -> 100 pp (max 1 multiples)
- Cuprium Component 0.1/min -> 100 pp (max 1 multiples)
- Hetonite Component 0.1/min -> 100 pp (max 1 multiples)
- Xiranite Component 1/min -> 20 pp (max 1 multiples)
- Cuprium Component 1/min -> 20 pp (max 1 multiples)
- Hetonite Component 1/min -> 20 pp (max 1 multiples)
